Our origin story

Built for life at 1%.

VoltEdge started with a familiar campus problem: a dying phone, a tangled charging cable, and nowhere convenient to plug in. Mike Boynton, Emmanuel Salami, and Andrew Rabena imagined a better way to keep students connected throughout a busy day.

Together, we created a fictional tech-accessory brand focused on practical products with a clean, modern look. From wireless charging pads and portable power banks to USB-C fast charging cables, adjustable laptop stands, magnetic phone mounts, and 6-in-1 USB-C hubs, our lineup helps your setup work as hard as you do.

Our mission: Keep your devices powered, your workspace organized, and your day moving.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who are VoltEdge products designed for?

Students, commuters, and anyone who wants a more convenient way to charge devices and organize their workspace.

Will every accessory work with my device?

Compatibility depends on your device. Check its charging features, ports, and connection requirements before choosing an accessory.

Why the name VoltEdge?

“Volt” represents power, and “Edge” represents the advantage of staying connected and prepared.
